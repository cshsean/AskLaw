Billable time has a habit of disappearing in the gaps — the email you answered on the train, the five-minute call between hearings, the quick read-through of a filing before you forwarded it on.

PointOne reads your time entries, correspondence and calendar, and pulls that scattered activity into draft invoice lines automatically. It doesn't guess at what happened in a meeting — it works from what's actually in your calendar and inbox metadata.

Nothing goes out to a client automatically. Every line PointOne drafts sits in a review queue. You check it, adjust anything that's off, and only then does it become part of an invoice. This review step isn't optional — it's firm policy, and it should never be skipped for speed.

Most firms find PointOne catches more billable time than manual logging alone, simply because it doesn't rely on remembering everything at the end of a long day.
