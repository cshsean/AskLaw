Meet PointOne — a billing assistant that quietly works in the background, built for billable time that just disappears. The email answered on the train. The five-minute call between hearings.

PointOne reads your calendar, correspondence and time entries, and turns that activity into draft invoice lines automatically — no guessing, just what's actually there.

But nothing goes straight to a client. Every line lands in a review queue first, and only after you check it does it become an invoice. That's firm policy — never skipped, however busy things get.

And the payoff: firms using PointOne consistently capture more billable time than logging it by hand.
