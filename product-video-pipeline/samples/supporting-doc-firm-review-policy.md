# Firm policy: human review of AI-drafted billing (sample supporting document)

Sample reference document for admin-UI testing — represents an internal firm
policy a reviewer might attach for context when approving a video's script.

## Policy
No AI-drafted invoice line reaches a client without a supervising associate
or partner reviewing and approving it individually. This applies regardless
of which billing tool is in use (PointOne, Biller, Ledgerline, or any future
tool added to the site).

## Why this matters for the video content
Any video describing an AI billing tool must show — not just say — a human
checkpoint before output reaches a client. This is a firm requirement, not
just a nice-to-have, and should be treated as non-negotiable content for
scene review (see `videoScript` scene 4 in `sample-script.json`).

## Client communication
Clients are told, in engagement letters, that AI-assisted tools may be used
to prepare draft billing and that all bills are reviewed by a lawyer before
issue. No video or explainer content should imply otherwise.
